# Chromamancer Voice and Tone

## Brand Voice

Chromamancer's voice is:
- **Mystical**: We use theme-appropriate magical terminology
- **Technical**: We're precise about color transformations
- **Helpful**: We guide users through the transmutation process

## Tone Guidelines

### In the CLI
- Clear and concise
- Use terms like "transmute", "transform", "convert"
- Provide helpful error messages with actionable solutions

### In the Extension
- Welcoming and approachable
- Guide users step-by-step
- Celebrate successful conversions

### In Documentation
- Thorough and precise
- Include examples
- Use metaphors that align with the magical theme where appropriate

## Terminology

- **Transmute**: Convert a theme from one format to another
- **Chromamancer**: One who practices the art of color transformation
- **Palette**: Color scheme
